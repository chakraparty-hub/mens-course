Module 3

The 7 Warning Patterns You'll Learn to Catch

A pattern seen early is a pattern avoided. A pattern rationalized is a pattern repeated.

Gold Digger

Oriented to your resources. Watch what she celebrates — and what her questions drift toward.

Controller

Starts small, escalates. Every test you pass by giving in teaches her the next one works.

Drama Engine

A storm follows her everywhere — and she needs you to rescue her from it.

Fixer-Upper

You're her project, not her partner. The job is never done — and you're the fuel.

Love Bomber

Perfect too fast. Future-faking compresses the timeline so you commit before the evidence.

Status Hunter

Drawn to the image of you. Listen to whether her answers are about you — or your life.

Exit Plan

She chose your trajectory, not you. When it stalls, so does she.
